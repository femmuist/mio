FAQ

How long does a commission take?

Most cards are done in about 5 to 7 days once your order and character details are in. Card + art takes a bit longer because I generate and polish the art, usually 7 to 10 days. If anything changes, I'll message you.

What can't I commission?

I won't write anything sexual involving characters under 18, real people, or anything illegal. If you're not sure your idea is a fit, message me on Discord before you order and I'll let you know.

Can I get a refund?

Every card is custom digital work, so sales are final once I've started. If something is wrong with your files or I can't deliver, message me and I'll fix it or refund you.

Can I reuse or share my card?

Your card is yours for personal use. You can edit it, use it in SillyTavern and Janitor, and share chats made with it. Please don't resell or redistribute the card files or the art, or pass them off as your own. Credit is always appreciated.
